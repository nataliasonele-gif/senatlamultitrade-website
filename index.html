About Senatla Multitrade

Our Vision

To be a leading multi-disciplinary company transforming South African communities through quality services and sustainable development.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional services in construction, plumbing, electrical work, mining support, and security while empowering youth through skills and entrepreneurship development.

BBBEE Positioning

Committed to Black Economic Empowerment principles, supporting local communities and creating employment opportunities for previously disadvantaged individuals.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of quality in all our work while actively contributing to community development and social upliftment.
